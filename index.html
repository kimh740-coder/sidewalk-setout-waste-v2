What it counts

Every 311 service request in 2025 coded Obstruction / Trash or Recycling, grouped by the community board the caller's address falls in. 1,446 in CD 2.

What it cannot show

Actual blocked sidewalks. 311 counts reports, so it rises with how many people walk by, own phones and bother to report. CD 2 has heavy foot traffic, so a high count is partly a high-attention count. Not population-adjusted.

Counting choice

Calendar 2025, the last complete year. One complaint type only: "Dirty Condition" and "Illegal Dumping" are excluded even though some describe the same bags.

What it counts

My own field survey: six sidewalk sites, each graded A (clear) to C (poor) for the environment of the setout area and for sidewalk obstruction.

What it cannot show

Anything beyond one visit per site. Grades are my judgment on a single day and time, so they cannot show change over the week or setout timing. Six sites cannot stand for all of CD 2.

Counting choice

Split grades (A-B, B-C) are plotted halfway between letters. Grades are ordinal, so a dot plot is used, not bars.

What it counts

The hour each 2025 CD 2 trash-obstruction request was created. 6–9 AM holds 1,342 of 1,446 reports (93%).

What it cannot show

When bags are actually on the sidewalk, or who reports. The spike may come from a small number of frequent reporters on their morning walk: the 1,446 reports cover only 455 distinct addresses. It cannot show setout time.

Counting choice

The 6, 7 and 8 AM hours are shown on their own. The other 21 hours of the day are combined into one gray bar (104 reports).

What it counts

The resolution text DSNY attached to each of the 1,446 CD 2 trash-obstruction requests in 2025, grouped by exact wording.

What it cannot show

Whether the sidewalk was cleared anyway. "Doesn't meet criteria" may mean bags were set out legally, in the allowed window, not that nothing was there. Outcomes for a bag do not show what the resident or business did next.

Counting choice

Six largest wordings shown as written (shortened); the 27 remaining reports are grouped as "Other outcomes". Ordered by count, not by severity.
